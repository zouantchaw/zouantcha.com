I spent much of a day in March setting up Obsidian. I had been using Logseq for about four or five years, and the graph had accumulated a lot of notes.

Logseq had become slower over the previous two weeks. That happened around the time I enabled its HTTP server, but I couldn’t say whether one caused the other.

Obsidian felt lighter. My first reaction was that it was faster. Then I remembered that I was comparing a fresh vault with years of accumulated data.

I was also starting to use coding agents alongside my notes. That was part of the reason for moving. It meant I expected the vault to grow more quickly than before, which made the fresh-vault comparison even less useful.

The thing I wanted to know was how it would feel after the notes had accumulated again. A good first afternoon wasn’t going to answer that.
