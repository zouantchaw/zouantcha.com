In March, I was reading *Basic Economics* by Thomas Sowell. The progress indicator said 52%. I started wondering how much of that half I could actually explain.

The day before, I had read a note about writing to remember what you read. So I stopped and tried it with that morning’s reading:

1. What were the main ideas?
2. Why did I care about them?
3. How did they support or contradict something I already thought?

The third question was the useful one. I noticed that I had an opinion about a subject I hadn’t really understood. Writing it down made that harder to ignore.

I’ve kept [reading lists](/bookshelf) for years. I like having a record of the books. But a list doesn’t tell me what I retained, or whether a book changed my mind. That needs a second pass.
