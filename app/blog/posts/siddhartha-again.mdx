I picked up my Kindle instead of my phone and started reading *Siddhartha* again. This is my second time. I’m highlighting things and trying to absorb more of it.

I liked the part where his father lets him leave. Whether he finds what he’s looking for or comes back disappointed, he can return and tell his father what happened.

I love that. There’s room to come back either way.
