There’s always construction in Montréal. I wanted a map where I could find out what was happening instead of just coming across another blocked street.

I started looking into it and read about New York’s open-data initiative. Then I found that Montréal already had a construction map. It was pretty much what I had wanted to build.

That search led me to the city’s mobility data and then to the [Montréal open data portal](https://donnees.montreal.ca/). There was a lot more there to look through.
