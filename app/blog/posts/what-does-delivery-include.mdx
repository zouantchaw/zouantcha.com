title: What does delivery include?
publishedAt: '2026-09-10'
writtenAt: '2026-03-23'
summary: A rental delivery exposed a question the booking flow hadn’t answered.
topic: software
---

A customer expected the rental items to be carried farther inside. We had understood the delivery differently.

I talked through it afterward. Where does delivery stop? When does it become setup? What if there’s a long distance to carry everything?

We need to make that clearer in the booking flow. Ask about the distance and what the customer needs before the delivery, rather than trying to agree on it at the door.
