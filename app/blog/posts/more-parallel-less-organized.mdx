I expected large language models to help me become more organized. In May, I wrote down that I still felt unorganized.

I could work on several things at the same time. But how well was I working on each one? What was the opportunity cost?

That was the whole note. I still think the question is more useful than treating the number of things in progress as a sign that I’m doing well.
